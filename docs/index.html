Buzz Drill

Quizbowl practice: tossups read word by word, bonuses, buzzpoints, and stats you can combine with friends.

iPhone or iPad

Open Buzz Drill
  1. Tap Open Buzz Drill above (in Safari).
  2. Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen.

Apple only allows App Store apps to install directly, so this is how free iPhone apps outside the App Store work. It opens full screen and works offline.

Android

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Rather not download a file? Open it in Chrome and tap Install app at the top.

On a computer?

Scan this with your phone's camera to install there, or use Buzz Drill in this browser.
